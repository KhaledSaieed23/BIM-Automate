R&D

Research & Development

New technologies are explored, tested against real BIM workflows, and — when proven — developed into usable tools within the platform.

Focus Areas

Applying machine learning, constraint-based logic, and automation techniques to solve problems that currently require significant manual effort in BIM workflows.

01

Problem Definition & Domain Analysis

Every initiative starts with a deep understanding of real-world challenges. We collaborate with domain specialists to analyze workflows, identify bottlenecks, and define the core problem from an engineering perspective.

This phase ensures that every research direction is grounded in practical needs, not assumptions.

02

Applied Research & Exploration

Once the problem is clearly defined, we initiate targeted research to explore potential solutions. This includes investigating computational methods, AI techniques, and algorithmic approaches relevant to the problem.

Feasibility, limitations, and performance are evaluated through controlled experiments before any development begins.

03

Development & Prototyping

Validated research outcomes are translated into functional prototypes and automation workflows. At this stage, we focus on building scalable, efficient solutions that integrate seamlessly with BIM environments.

All development maintains alignment with engineering standards and project requirements throughout the build cycle.
